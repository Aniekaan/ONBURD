Frequently asked
questions

Everything you need to feel confident before you hit publish. Still unsure? Talk to our team.

Is it free to get started?

Yes. Creating your account and setting up your event is completely free — you only pay a small fee per ticket sold. No setup costs and no monthly charges.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts are fast and secure. Your earnings settle to your account on a regular schedule, and you can track every naira in your dashboard as tickets sell.

Can I set different ticket types and prices?

Absolutely. Create as many tiers as you like — Regular, VIP, early-bird, table packages — each with its own price, perks and quantity.

What fees does Onburd charge?

A small, transparent per-ticket fee. You'll always see the exact breakdown before you publish — no hidden charges, ever.

Can I manage attendees and check people in?

Yes. You get a live attendee list and fast QR check-in at the door, so entry stays smooth even for a full house.

What if I need to change or cancel my event?

You're in control. Update details anytime, and if plans change we'll help you handle refunds cleanly and keep your guests informed.
